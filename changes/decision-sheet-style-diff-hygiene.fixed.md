- **decision-sheet and style-diff stop dropping things silently.** A sidecar finding with the same `check` as a proposal but on another page now gets its own card instead of being skipped; `--parse` warns on stderr about any `DECISION` line it cannot read (for example no `|` after the option) and exits 1, and `--parse --against <decisions-*.json>` drops answers for unknown cards or options a card does not offer; `--date` and `--label` are restricted to `[A-Za-z0-9._-]+` without `..` so they cannot escape `--out`; and `style-diff` records the HTTP status and final URL of each page, so a 4xx/5xx page or a different final URL between snapshots is UNMEASURED (exit 3) rather than CLEAN.
